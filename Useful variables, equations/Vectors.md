#Useful 
>[!info] +, - vectors
>$$ |+\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt 2} \Big (|0\rangle+|1\rangle \Big ) $$
>$$ |-\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2} \Big (|0\rangle-|1\rangle\Big ) $$
>Then
>$$
|0\rangle =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }} (|+\rangle +|-\rangle ),\quad |1\rangle =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }} (|+\rangle -|-\rangle  )$$

>[!info] Bell States
>$$\begin{aligned} |\Phi_{+}\rangle &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(|00\rangle+|11\rangle) \\|\Phi_{-}\rangle &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(|00\rangle-|11\rangle) \\|\Psi_{+}\rangle &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(|01\rangle + |10\rangle)\\|\Psi_{-}\rangle&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(|01\rangle-|10\rangle)\end{aligned}$$
